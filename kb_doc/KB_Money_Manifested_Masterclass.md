## [MM-06] The Money Goal Exercise and the 5x Provocation

**Context:** An early live exercise Sanaya runs to expose a student's ceiling on receiving.

**Step 1.** Ask: what is your current money goal? Write a number that feels right to you right now. It can be ₹1 lakh a month, ₹10 lakhs a month, ₹1 crore a month, ₹10 crore. There is no judgment — this is a safe space, and the more responsive people are, the better the energy of the group.

**Step 2 — the provocation.** Now, what if you were to **2x** that number? Actually, forget 2x. What if you were to **5x** it? If your goal was 2 lakhs, make it 10 lakhs. If your goal was 1 crore, make it 5 crore. **How does that make you feel?**

**Step 3 — read the reaction.** The answers that come back are usually: *doubting it, it feels unrealistic, it feels unbelievable, are you joking?* — and occasionally *love it*.

**Why this matters.** That reaction is not about the number. It is a direct readout of **how open you are to receiving money.** The resistance you feel at 5x is the exact edge we spend the rest of the session working on.

I return to this at the end of every class: **whatever goal you had at the beginning, you are going to 5x it.**

**Tags:** money goal exercise, 5x your goal, doubling the number, openness to receiving, ceiling on receiving, live exercise, resistance readout

**Q: How do I attract earning opportunities when no ideas come to mind right now?**

**Q: I am financially dependent on my husband or family. How do I manifest the desire to do something of my own?**
A: Set the intention and talk to the universe. We all start from somewhere. Use statements like: *"I desire to do something. I desire to be financially independent. I am available to start a new venture. I have the time and the resources to start a new venture. Everyone is so supportive of my new venture."*

**Q: How do I attract a specific thing — luxury travel, a dream wedding, jewellery, a car, a house?**
A: **Get specific on what you want, then go deeper into why you want it.** How is it going to enrich the quality of your life? For a dream house: how is that house going to make you feel? Is it a better area? More space to do what you want? More privacy for everyone? **What are the emotions attached to having that house?** The specificity plus the emotional why is what makes it land.

**Q: I get a positive intuitive thought and then a conflicting thought comes. How do I handle it?**
A: **Let the conflicting thought come.** You just have to be aware that it *is* a conflicting thought. **Awareness is the first step towards removing it.**

It is personalised to you and to your authentic being. Ask yourself *do I desire this?* and watch how your body responds at a somatic level. Sample the variety of experiences available and notice which ones land.

**Q: How do I overcome jealousy?**

If I can see it, it is available for me.* Send as much loving, positive energy to that person as you can, and be grateful that you get to experience it just by watching them. **Q: I am working on my new venture and I do not see results yet.**
A: **Devotion, consistency, commitment, action. Keep at it** — while keeping all of your desires in mind. **The universe is the friendliest, kindest, most loving entity in the world, and it is here to support you. It always has your back.** You just have to make yourself available to it.
